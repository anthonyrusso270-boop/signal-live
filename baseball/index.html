How to read it

Prices are Bovada's American odds; implied is the vig-inclusive probability and fair removes the overround proportionally. Move is the change in a side's implied probability since the first snapshot on file. Grading uses the MLB Stats API box score: the full-game moneyline is settled from the closing favourite's side, totals push at the number, the first-five moneyline pushes on a tie and the 3-way tie contract settles on its own. Closing line = last snapshot before first pitch; CLV = closing minus opening implied for the settled side. Calibration compares the closing fair probability of the favourite with how often the favourite actually won: a market that prices correctly sits on the diagonal. There are no picks on this page; it is the record any model has to beat.
